  ## 📚 Part 2: How to Get & Prepare Datasets for CampusMind

  To train/ingest real campus data into ChromaDB, follow these steps:

  ### 1. Types of Data to Collect

  Collect official campus text documents across these categories:

  • Academic Circulars: Fee payment dates, registration deadlines, exam schedules.
  • Rules & Handbooks: Library borrowing limits, hostel rules, code of conduct, grading policy.
  • Department Notices: Syllabus guides, placement notices, internship guidelines.
  • Faculty Notices: Research grant forms, conference funding rules, lab safety requirements.
  ──────
  ### 2. How to Source Real Campus Datasets

  #### Option A: Extract from your University Website / PDFs

  If your university publishes PDFs or web pages:

  • PDF Extraction: Convert PDF notices into plain text using Python (pdfplumber or pypdf):
    import pdfplumber

    with pdfplumber.open("academic_calendar_2026.pdf") as pdf:
        text = "\n".join([page.extract_text() for page in pdf.pages])

    with open("../data/raw/academic_calendar_2026.txt", "w", encoding="utf-8") as f:
        f.write(text)


  #### Option B: Download Public Datasets (Kaggle & GitHub)

  Search for higher education / university datasets:

  • Kaggle: Search University FAQs dataset, College admission queries, or Campus notice dataset.
  • HuggingFace Datasets: Search for academic QA datasets such as squad or higher education QA subsets.

  #### Option C: Generate Synthetic Campus Datasets with LLMs

  You can generate clean text circulars using ChatGPT / Gemini with prompts like:

  │ "Generate a 500-word official university circular covering exam timetables, hostel nightout rules, library fines, and placement
  │ registration deadlines for 2026."
  ──────
  ### 3. Adding New Datasets to CampusMind

  1. Place your text files inside :
    campus-ai-assistant/data/raw/
    ├── sample_campus_rules.txt
    ├── library_handbook_2026.txt
    ├── placement_notice_2026.txt
    └── faculty_research_policy.txt

  2. Role Scope Naming Conventions (for automatic RBAC tagging):
      • Files containing "faculty" in their filename (e.g., faculty_research_policy.txt) are tagged for faculty, admin access only.
      • Files containing "admin" in their filename are tagged for admin access only.
      • All other files are readable by student, faculty, admin.
  3. Re-run the Ingestion Pipeline:
    cd C:\Users\pc\Downloads\campus-ai-assistant\backend
    python -m rag.ingest
    ChromaDB will automatically chunk, embed, and update its vector index!
