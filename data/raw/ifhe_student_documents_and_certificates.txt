IFHE HYDERABAD (ICFAI FOUNDATION FOR HIGHER EDUCATION)
STUDENT CERTIFICATES, OFFICIAL BONAFIDE DOCUMENTS, VERIFICATION & ID CARD POLICY

1. OFFICIAL BONAFIDE CERTIFICATES & ISSUANCE WORKFLOW
- Bonafide Certificates are official documents issued by the Academic Registry certifying a student's active enrollment, academic year, and discipline.
- Types of Bonafide Certificates & Standard Processing Times:
  * Educational Bank Loan Bonafide: Issued in 24-48 hours (includes fee structure breakdown).
  * Passport / Police Verification Bonafide: Issued in 48 hours.
  * Bus & Railway Concession Bonafide: Issued in 24 hours.
  * Visa / Travel Concession Bonafide: Issued in 48 hours.
  * Summer/Winter Internship No-Objection Certificate (NOC): Issued in 48 hours upon department head approval.
- How to Apply: Log into IFHE Student ERP -> Student Services -> Request Certificate -> Select Purpose & Upload supporting docs (if required). Standard fee: Free for first copy, INR 100 for duplicate copy.

2. CONDUCT, CHARACTER, TRANSFER & MIGRATION CERTIFICATES
- Conduct & Character Certificate: Issued automatically upon degree completion certifying moral conduct and adherence to academic regulations.
- Transfer Certificate (TC) & Migration Certificate (MC):
  * Issued to graduating students or students withdrawing from the university.
  * Prerequisites: Submission of completed No-Dues Clearance Certificate signed by Library, Accounts, Hostel Warden, Sports Department, and Department HOD.
  * Processing time: 3 business days post No-Dues submission. Fee: INR 300.

3. SMART RFID STUDENT ID CARD REPLACEMENT & PRIVILEGES
- The IFHE Smart Student ID Card serves as campus access pass, library checkout token, exam hall entry pass, and hostel mess access card.
- Lost or Damaged ID Card Replacement:
  * Step 1: File an online Lost Card Report on IFHE Student ERP or submit a written application at Security Desk.
  * Step 2: Pay ID Card Replacement Fee of INR 500 online or at the Accounts Counter.
  * Step 3: Temporary gate pass is issued immediately; new RFID Smart ID Card is printed and dispatched within 24 hours by the IT & Security Helpdesk.

4. WES ATTestation, DEGREE APOSTILLE & THIRD-PARTY VERIFICATION
- WES (World Education Services) & Foreign Credential Attestation:
  * IFHE is officially registered on WES Access for direct electronic document transmission.
  * Students can submit WES reference number via ERP for direct digital transcript dispatch.
- Third-Party Employer / Background Verification:
  * Employers and background verification agencies must send verification requests to backgroundverif@ifheindia.org along with student consent form and payment of INR 1,000 verification fee. Standard turnaround time: 3-5 business days.
