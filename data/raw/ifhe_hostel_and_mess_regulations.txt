IFHE HYDERABAD (ICFAI FOUNDATION FOR HIGHER EDUCATION)
CAMPUS HOSTEL & MESS RULES AND SAFETY REGULATIONS

1. HOSTEL ENTRY & CURFEW TIMINGS
- Campus In-Gate Curfew: All hostel residents must return to the university campus by 8:30 PM on weekdays (Monday through Friday) and by 9:00 PM on weekends/holidays.
- Hostel Block Curfew: Biometric attendance in respective hostel blocks is strictly recorded between 9:00 PM and 9:45 PM daily.
- Late Entry Violations: Late entries without prior approved Out-Pass will attract a fine of INR 500 for the first offense, INR 1,000 for the second offense, and formal parent notification plus disciplinary committee hearing for subsequent violations.

2. OUT-PASS & LEAVE PROCEDURE
- Local Out-Pass: Available via the IFHE Campus Mobile App / Student Portal for day outings between 6:00 AM and 8:30 PM.
- Outstation Leave Pass: Requires online approval from the Chief Warden at least 24 hours in advance, backed by parent confirmation via registered SMS/email.

3. DINING HALL & MESS COMMITTEE GUIDELINES
- Meal Timings:
  - Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 9:15 AM
  - Lunch: 12:15 PM – 2:15 PM
  - Evening Snacks: 4:30 PM – 5:45 PM
  - Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM
- The IFHE Mess Committee comprises student representatives, wardens, and nutritionists who audit food quality, hygiene standards, and weekly menu rotation. Food waste is monitored; excessive food wastage carries a warning.

4. SAFETY, DISCIPLINE & ANTI-RAGGING ZERO TOLERANCE
- Ragging in any form (physical, verbal, or mental) is strictly prohibited under the UGC Regulations on Curbing Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions. Violators face immediate suspension, expulsion, and police FIR filing under anti-ragging laws.
- Substance Possession Prohibition: Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes, or illicit substances on campus or in hostels carries immediate expulsion and legal prosecution.
- Visitors Policy: Parents and authorized guardians may meet residents at the Central Visitors Lounge between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Visitors are not allowed inside resident rooms under any circumstances.
