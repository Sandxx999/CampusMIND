IFHE HYDERABAD (ICFAI FOUNDATION FOR HIGHER EDUCATION)
EXAMINATION SCHEDULES, TIMETABLES, HALL TICKET ISSUANCE & ADMIT CARD REGULATIONS (2026)

1. EXAMINATION CALENDAR & TIMETABLE (SPRING & FALL 2026)
- Mid-Term Examinations (Spring 2026): Conducted April 10, 2026 – April 20, 2026.
- End-Semester Examinations (Spring 2026): Conducted May 25, 2026 – June 15, 2026.
- Supplementary & Make-up Examinations: Conducted July 1, 2026 – July 10, 2026.
- Daily Examination Slot Timings:
  * Morning Session (Slot A): 09:30 AM – 12:30 PM (Gates open at 09:00 AM, last entry allowed: 10:00 AM)
  * Afternoon Session (Slot B): 02:00 PM – 05:00 PM (Gates open at 01:30 PM, last entry allowed: 02:30 PM)
- Detailed course-wise date sheets are published on the Student ERP Portal 3 weeks before exams.

2. HALL TICKET (ADMIT CARD) ISSUANCE & DOWNLOAD PROCEDURE
- Official Hall Tickets are generated on the IFHE Student ERP Portal (https://ifheindia.org/erp) 10 days prior to the start of examinations.
- Download Schedule:
  * Mid-Term Hall Tickets: Available for download from April 1, 2026.
  * End-Semester Hall Tickets: Available for download from May 15, 2026.
- Mandatory Hall Ticket Eligibility Requirements:
  1. Minimum 75% aggregate attendance in each registered subject.
  2. 100% clearance of semester tuition fees, hostel mess dues, and library fines.
  3. No active disciplinary sanctions, honor code holds, or pending administrative inquiries.

3. PHYSICAL HALL TICKET MANDATE & EXAM HALL ENTRY RULES
- Students MUST present a clear physical printout of their Hall Ticket along with their official physical IFHE RFID Student ID Card at the exam hall. Digital copies on mobile phones are STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
- Invigilators will verify barcode, candidate signature, and photo on the Hall Ticket.
- Emergency Duplicate Hall Ticket: If lost or damaged on exam day, a temporary emergency hall ticket can be issued at the Exam Control Office (Block C, Room 102) upon payment of a duplicate fee of INR 200.
- Photo Mismatch/Discrepancy: Any correction on the printed hall ticket must be certified by the Controller of Examinations at least 48 hours prior to exam start.

4. SEATING ALLOCATION, CLASH RESOLUTION & SPECIAL ACCOMMODATION
- Seating Arrangement: Hall seating lists are posted 2 hours prior to every session on the Central Exam Control Notice Board and pushed to the IFHE Mobile App.
- Exam Clash Protocol: If a candidate has two conflicting exams scheduled in the same time slot, they must report to the Exam Control Office 5 days prior for allocation of consecutive slots under supervision.
- Scribe & Accommodation Policy for PwD Candidates: Differently-abled candidates requiring a scribe or extra time (20 minutes extra per hour) must register medical certificates with the Academic Council 10 days before exams.
