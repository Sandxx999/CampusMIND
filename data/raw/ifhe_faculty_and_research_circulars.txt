IFHE HYDERABAD (ICFAI FOUNDATION FOR HIGHER EDUCATION)
FACULTY RESEARCH GRANTS, WORKLOAD & ADMINISTRATIVE CIRCULAR

1. FACULTY RESEARCH SEED GRANT SCHEME
- Full-time faculty members at IFHE (IcfaiTech, IBS, Law School) are eligible for Seed Research Grants up to INR 3,00,000 for innovative research proposals approved by the University Research Council (URC).
- Publication Requirement: Grant awardees must publish at least two research papers in Scopus-indexed or Web of Science (WoS) Q1/Q2 journals within 18 months of grant disbursement.

2. TEACHING WORKLOAD & CONSULTANCY POLICY
- Full Professors: Minimum 8 direct teaching contact hours per week + active Ph.D. supervision.
- Associate Professors: Minimum 12 direct teaching contact hours per week + active research projects.
- Assistant Professors: Minimum 16 direct teaching contact hours per week + lab supervision.
- Consultancy Share: Faculty consulting revenue generated through industry projects is shared in a 70:30 ratio (70% to faculty investigator, 30% to IFHE Institutional Infrastructure Fund).

3. SPONSORED RESEARCH & CONFERENCES
- Travel Grant: Full-time faculty are eligible for 100% financial assistance once per financial year to present peer-reviewed papers at premier international conferences indexed by IEEE, ACM, or Scopus.
- Intellectual Property (IPR) & Patent Filing: Legal costs for filing national and international patents originating from IFHE labs are fully sponsored by the IFHE Centre for Innovation & IPR Cell.
