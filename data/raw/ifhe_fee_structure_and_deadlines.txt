IFHE HYDERABAD (ICFAI FOUNDATION FOR HIGHER EDUCATION)
OFFICIAL FEE STRUCTURE, PAYMENT DEADLINES, SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL REGULATIONS

1. SEMESTER TUITION FEE STRUCTURE (2026 ACADEMIC YEAR)
- B.Tech (Computer Science / AI & ML / Data Science / ECE): INR 1,25,000 per semester.
- BBA / BBA-LLB (Honours): INR 95,000 per semester.
- BA-LLB / BBA-LLB (5-Year Law Programs): INR 1,10,000 per semester.
- MBA (ICFAI Business School - IBS Hyderabad): INR 2,25,000 per semester.
- Ph.D. Research Scholar Program: INR 45,000 per semester.

2. PAYMENT DEADLINES & LATE FEE PENALTY RULES
- Fall Semester Fee Due Date: July 15, 2026 (Grace period until July 25, 2026).
- Spring Semester Fee Due Date: December 15, 2026 (Grace period until December 25, 2026).
- Late Fee Penalty Structure:
  * Payment between 1 to 10 days post grace period: INR 100 per day fine.
  * Payment between 11 to 25 days post grace period: INR 250 per day fine.
  * Payment after 25 days: Student registration is suspended, portal access revoked, and debarred from attending classes/writing exams.
- Online Payment Modes: Net Banking, Credit/Debit Cards, UPI, or NEFT/RTGS via IFHE Fee Portal (https://ifheindia.org/pay). Cash/Cheques are not accepted.

3. HOSTEL & MESS FEE SCHEDULE
- AC Double Sharing Accommodation: INR 65,000 per semester.
- Non-AC Double Sharing Accommodation: INR 45,000 per semester.
- Mandatory Mess Advance: INR 35,000 per semester (includes 4 meals daily).
- Refundable Security Deposit: INR 10,000 (one-time refundable upon graduation).

4. MERIT SCHOLARSHIP & FEE CONCESSION POLICY
- Merit Scholarship based on Semester CGPA:
  * Top 5% of class (CGPA >= 9.0): 50% tuition fee waiver for subsequent semester.
  * Next 5% of class (CGPA 8.5 – 8.99): 25% tuition fee waiver.
- Sports & Extracurricular Scholarship: 25% fee waiver for national/state level players approved by Sports Committee.
