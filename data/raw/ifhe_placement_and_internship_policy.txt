IFHE HYDERABAD (ICFAI FOUNDATION FOR HIGHER EDUCATION)
CAMPUS PLACEMENT POLICY, INTERNSHIP GUIDELINES & CAREER DEVELOPMENT RULES

1. CAMPUS PLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY & REGISTRATION
- Placement Registration: Final year B.Tech, BBA, Law, and MBA students must register with the Career Development Center (CDC) by August 1, 2026.
- Registration Fee: One-time non-refundable CDC career service fee of INR 1,500.
- Academic Eligibility Criteria:
  * Minimum CGPA of 6.0 across completed semesters.
  * Tier-1 Dream Companies (CTC >= INR 10.0 LPA): CGPA >= 7.5 with 0 active backlogs.
  * Tier-2 Companies (CTC INR 5.0 – 9.9 LPA): Maximum 1 active backlog permitted at the time of recruitment process.

2. ONE-STUDENT ONE-OFFER POLICY & DREAM OFFER EXCEPTION
- Standard Rule: Once a student receives a formal job offer through campus placement, they are de-registered from subsequent campus placement drives.
- Dream Offer Exception: A student securing an offer < INR 8.0 LPA is eligible to apply for ONE "Dream Tier Company" offering CTC >= INR 12.0 LPA. If selected, the student must accept the Dream Offer.

3. SUMMER & WINTER INTERNSHIP POLICY (SIP / WIP)
- Internship Duration: Mandatory 8-12 week summer internship program between 6th and 7th semester for B.Tech/BBA, and 12-week SIP for MBA.
- Pre-Placement Offers (PPO): Students receiving a PPO during summer internship must intimate CDC within 7 days of receipt. PPO acceptance is binding.
- Attendance & Academic Exemption: Students participating in official off-campus placement interviews or internship drives are granted official duty leave (OD) upon CDC coordinator authorization.
