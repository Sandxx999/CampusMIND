=====================================================
OFFICIAL CAMPUS CIRCULAR: SPRING 2026 ACADEMIC RULES & TIMETABLE
=====================================================

1. FEE PAYMENT DEADLINES & POLICIES
- The final deadline for Spring 2026 tuition fee payment is March 15, 2026.
- A flat late fee penalty of $50 per week applies for payments made after March 15, 2026.
- Students with outstanding dues beyond April 1, 2026, will be barred from mid-term examinations.

2. LIBRARY BORROWING & RULES
- Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for a duration of 14 days.
- Postgraduate students and faculty members may borrow up to 10 books for 30 days.
- Overdue fine for standard books is $1.00 per day per item.
- Reference books and rare journal volumes cannot be checked out and must be read within the main reading room.

3. EXAM TIMETABLE & GENERAL NOTICES
- Mid-Term Examinations will take place between April 10, 2026, and April 20, 2026.
- Final Examinations are scheduled from June 1, 2026, to June 15, 2026.
- Hall tickets will be downloadable from the student portal starting April 1, 2026.
- Attendance requirement: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all registered subjects to be eligible for final examinations.

4. FACULTY RESEARCH CIRCULAR (FACULTY & ADMIN ROLE SCOPE ONLY)
- Faculty research grant applications for Academic Year 2026-2027 close on April 30, 2026.
- Maximum research funding grant per department is capped at $25,000.
- All lab safety audits must be completed prior to submitting grant applications.
